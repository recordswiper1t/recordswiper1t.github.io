- Timer measures wall-clock seconds from launch until every active wave has finished spawning and the final living enemy is killed

- Underwater transformation enemies and the final-boss fusion piece keep their intended internal transformations; their resulting normal enemies still recycle at the map exit
- Recycled enemies keep the same object, health, and boss state and restart at path node 0
- Existing V6 sandbox, hero, boss Send All, and performance patches remain intact
