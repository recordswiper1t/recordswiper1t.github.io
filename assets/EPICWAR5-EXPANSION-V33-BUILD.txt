Performance-first changes:
- Character collision/target scans use a generous X-axis broad phase before Flash hit-test calls.
- Unit depth placement uses incremental insertion rather than allocating/sorting the entire battlefield on every spawn.
- Health bars, player army counters and wave counts update at lower staggered rates while simulation timing stays per-frame.
- Enemy-wave counting no longer reissues movement orders to every wave unit during each count scan.
- Expansion disposable-wave concurrency is capped at 16-22 per wave controller while bosses/elites remain intact.
- Rain/blood-rain and snow particle budgets are sharply reduced and scale down further when adaptive quality engages.
- Off-camera/transient hit effects, damage text and blood splats are culled/capped.
- BattleSystem automatically drops Flash render quality only under very heavy display-list load and restores it when load settles.

Quality/fix changes:
- Repairs V3/V3.2 Expansion relic ownership values so earned first-clear gear is actually selectable.
- Future first-clear Expansion gear is stored with the correct ownership value.
- Adds Field Standard at 45 unique clears and Conqueror Medal at 50 unique clears.
- Equipment catalog grows from 55 to 57 entries (+3.6%), safely below the requested 10% content-growth ceiling.
- No new stages, units, army slots or upgrade slots.
- V3.2 role-specific advanced trees, progression, gear tradeoffs and threat bands remain intact.
