What V3.1 improves

Better pacing
The Expansion campaign opens after the 12 story and 8 extra battles; trials remain parallel challenge content.
Upgrade economy
Advanced upgrades are still expensive, but no longer require absurd replay grinding to finish naturally.
Clearer progression
New army slots, gear slots, advanced upgrades, and the Expansion campaign announce themselves when unlocked.
Late-game balance
Defense stacking is capped below immunity and extreme population stacking is capped to keep armies strong but manageable.
Safer saving
Battle results now flush progression immediately, and expansion save arrays are repaired to the expected size when loaded.
Battle performance
12-slot player armies and enemy waves use cached unit counts instead of rescanning the whole battlefield every frame.

Progression curve

Army slots
Start at 3 and unlock up to 12 across unique battle clears.
Equipment
Start with 1 slot per character, later unlock 2 and then 3.
Upgrades
Each unit/hero grows from 9 original upgrade nodes to 18 total.
Campaign
25 original battles plus the same 25-stage Expansion campaign.
Gear
The same 55-item catalog; V3.1 does not add more equipment.
Enemies
The same expansion encounters and bosses, with player power kept under control so they remain meaningful.

How it unlocks now

Unique battle clears still drive your power curve. Army capacity climbs from 3 to 12, the second and third equipment slots arrive later, and advanced upgrades unlock after 18 unique clears. The Expansion campaign now opens once all 12 story stages and all 8 extra stages are cleared; the five trial battles can be tackled alongside the Expansion instead of acting as a mandatory gate.

V3.1 is intended as an in-place polish update to the Expansion. If you care about an existing run, use the Ruffle Save Manager once before switching versions so you have a backup.
