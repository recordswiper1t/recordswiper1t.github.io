Release verified
Exact V3.4 was patched, rebuilt, re-decompiled, checksum-recorded and checked against the frozen gameplay/save/performance invariants.

Performance work

Cheaper combat targeting
Character hit/target scans now use a generous spatial broad-phase before expensive Flash hit-tests.
Cheaper unit spawning
New units are inserted into depth order incrementally instead of allocating and sorting the entire battlefield on every spawn.
Less per-frame UI work
Health bars, army counters and wave counts refresh on staggered schedules while combat timers still run every frame.
Smarter wave bookkeeping
Counting enemy waves no longer reissues movement orders to every matching unit; orders refresh when units actually spawn.
Effect budgets
Rain, blood rain, snow, blood splats, damage text and transient hit effects are capped or culled when off-screen.
Adaptive heavy-load mode
Flash render quality drops only under very heavy display-list load and restores automatically once the battlefield settles.

Gameplay and reliability

Cleaner huge battles
Expansion disposable-wave concurrency is reduced to 16–22 per wave controller, while elites and bosses remain fully present.
Relic ownership fixed
Older Expansion saves repair first-clear relic values so gear you earned is actually selectable in the equipment screen.
Future rewards fixed
New first-clear Expansion relic rewards now use the game's real owned-item value instead of an unusable count of 1.
Field Standard
A new milestone relic unlocks at 45 unique clears: population +1, health +12%, damage +12%.
Conqueror Medal
A final 50-clear relic gives damage +18%, health +18%, defense +10%, speed +0.3.
V3.2 retained
Role-specific advanced trees, 45-clear 12th slot, threat bands, gear tradeoffs, save repair and progression feedback all remain.

Content ceiling

V3.5 retains V3.3's two additional equipment rewards: the catalog grows from 55 to 57 items, a 3.6% increase. V3.5 adds no stages, units, army slots or upgrade slots.

If an existing save matters to you, make one backup with the Ruffle Save Manager before changing versions. V3.1 through V3.4 remain archived in the repository.
