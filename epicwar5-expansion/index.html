What V3.2 improves

Progression finally lines up
Finishing the main story + extra + Expansion path naturally reaches all 12 army slots at 45 unique clears. Trials speed that up instead of secretly being required.
Real advanced identities
The 9 advanced nodes are now role-specific: tanks gain shields/walls/resists, casters gain spells, engineers gain towers, swarm units gain population tools, and damage units get stronger risk/reward paths.
Better upgrade pacing
Advanced branch roots, middle nodes, and capstones now cost 16× / 18× / 20× rank instead of every advanced node using the same price.
Gear choices, not one loadout
The strongest late items have clearer strengths and drawbacks, and one character cannot equip the exact same item in multiple equipment slots.
Better reward loop
Expansion equipment is a first-clear reward. Replaying a stage gives bonus EXP instead of turning the best gear into an infinite duplicate farm.
Smoother difficulty
Raw enemy HP/attack growth is less spiky, second portals/bosses arrive slightly later, and stages show T1–T5 threat bands so difficulty steps are visible.

Progression notes

The Expansion campaign still opens after all 12 story stages and 8 extra stages. Advanced upgrades still open after 18 unique clears. Army slot milestones now continue through the main path to a guaranteed 12th slot at 45 unique clears, while trial victories count as optional shortcuts. Stage replays are still worthwhile for EXP, but their unique equipment reward is tied to the first clear.

V3.2 is intended as an in-place quality update. If an existing save matters to you, make one backup with the Ruffle Save Manager before switching versions.
