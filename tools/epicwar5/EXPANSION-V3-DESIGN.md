# Epic War 5 — Expansion V3 direction

Goal: turn the mod into a progression expansion rather than a cheat build.

## Progression targets

- Start weaker than vanilla and finish substantially stronger.
- Army/unit slots unlock across the campaign. Target: start at 3 active unit slots, reach 12 by the late game/endgame (2x the vanilla six-slot ceiling).
- Equipment slots unlock per unit/hero: slot 1 at base, slot 2 in midgame, slot 3 in late game.
- Units and heroes gain a second upgrade tier/tree beyond the original ability set. The extra progression must be earned, not granted automatically.
- Campaign content target: 50 total stages/challenges versus the original 25, using a mix of remixed existing battlefields and genuinely new encounter definitions first; new visual map art can be layered in later without blocking gameplay content.
- New equipment pool should be broad and tiered, with early/mid/late/endgame drop and unlock bands.
- Enemy scaling must rise with the expanded power ceiling: stronger stat budgets, denser compositions, elite variants, boss remixes, and postgame encounters.

## Design rule

No permanent always-on cheat values. Sandbox/debug controls remain separate from campaign progression. Expansion mode should be playable normally from a fresh save.

## Milestone order

1. Map and extend army-slot storage/UI/battle consumption from 6 to 12, with campaign-based unlock count.
2. Map and extend equipment storage/UI/stat aggregation from 1 to 3 slots per unit/hero, with unlock thresholds.
3. Map original ability/skill storage and UI; add a second progression tier using data-driven stat modifiers and/or additional ability IDs where safe.
4. Raise stage storage/selection ceiling from 25 to 50 and add a second campaign page/region.
5. Add 25 expansion encounters with escalating enemy budgets and reward tables.
6. Add large tiered equipment catalogue and enemy/elite/boss scaling.
7. Balance pass so early game is harder/weaker than vanilla, midgame opens up, and endgame power is dramatically higher but necessary.

## Initial unlock curve proposal

- Stages 1–2: 3 unit slots, 1 equipment slot.
- Stage 3: 4th unit slot.
- Stage 6: 5th unit slot.
- Stage 10: 6th unit slot (roughly vanilla ceiling reached later than vanilla).
- Stage 14: 7th unit slot + equipment slot 2.
- Stage 18: 8th unit slot.
- Stage 22: 9th unit slot.
- Stage 27: 10th unit slot + equipment slot 3.
- Stage 34: 11th unit slot.
- Stage 42: 12th unit slot.
- Stages 43–50: true endgame tuned around 12 slots, 3 equipment slots, and expanded upgrades.

Thresholds are implementation defaults and can be tuned after playtesting.
