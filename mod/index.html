V12.2 highlights

  • The Last Rift: after the original Level 15 campaign boss, the level continues into a new 30-wave second act with three runtime-built routes and 15 additional tower sites.
  • New post-boss combat roster: evolved Rift enemy archetypes, support healing, enraging stalkers and the wave-30 Rift Sovereign boss.
  • Map allies: Nyra the Riftwarden and the Rift Beacon provide map-only support roles using safe embedded animation assets.
  • Loop/recycle records: persistent fastest completion, fewest virtual lives lost and combined score. Official bests require a complete run started before wave 1.
  • Exact tower blueprints: Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V preserves standard tower tier and permanent purchased tier-4 ability ranks where exposed by the SWF.
  • Sandbox lifecycle: menu pause safety, hero summon cleanup, sellable map specials, complete enemy catalog, cleanup controls and presets.
  • Performance: native launcher renderer fallback, allocation-conscious swarm logic, adaptive visual quality and configurable load thresholds while combat remains full-rate.
  • V12.2 reliability: current audio and pop-up fixes remain, while obsolete advertising/login startup dependencies no longer block the campaign.

Windows crash protection

The native launcher defaults to DirectX 12 and retries OpenGL if the selected renderer exits unsuccessfully. Vulkan is opt-in only. This avoids the wgpu/Vulkan panic seen on some Windows driver combinations.
