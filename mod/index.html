Best iPhone setup

In Safari, open this Mod Lab, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Launch it from the home-screen icon for the most app-like full-screen experience.

Your progress and SWF are separate. Replacing the SWF with a compatible modded copy does not intentionally erase the Ruffle SharedObject save.

Use ⋯ → Saves / backup and export a Ruffle save occasionally. Clearing Safari website data can remove local progress.

Laptop vs iPhone

Use the laptop for heavy work like decompiling and editing tower stats. Once a modded SWF is ready, you can AirDrop/save it to the iPhone and choose Use a modified SWF here. The iPhone remains the main play/test device.

Clean source: Internet Archive item kingdom-rush-frontie-15717. The mirrored SWF is served from /assets/kingdom-rush-frontie-15717.swf.
