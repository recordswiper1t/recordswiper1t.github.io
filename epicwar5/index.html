Epic War 5

Adjustable controls

F1 Add selected mana amount
[ / ] Halve / double mana amount (10–5000)
F11 Toggle free spells
F12 Toggle rapid unit production
B Toggle building cap 4 ↔ 999
P Toggle population boost
F2 / F3 Previous / next spawn unit
F4 / F5 Spawn ally / enemy
F6 Spawn batch 1 / 5 / 20 / 50
F7 Speed 1× / 4× / 8×
F8 Wipe enemies
F9 Instant win
F10 Heal all allies
` Hide / show sandbox HUD

The HUD shows which toggles are ON and your current mana/spawn amounts. Fast-production/population changes apply when unit spawners initialize, so toggle them before starting/restarting a battle for consistent behavior.

What changed from V1

  • No automatic 99,999,999 gold, unlock-all, stage completion, accessories, or EXP injection on load.
  • No automatic 9,999 mana or boosted mana regeneration.
  • Free spells, unlimited buildings, fast production, and population boost all start OFF.
  • Mana is additive and adjustable rather than a hard-coded fill.
  • Unit spawning and destructive tools remain manual actions only.
